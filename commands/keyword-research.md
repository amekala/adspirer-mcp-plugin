This command has moved. Ask directly — "find keywords for my plumbing business" — and the
`adspirer-google-ads` skill runs keyword research through the `google_ads` router, with live CPC
data from Google's Keyword Planner.

For the search terms already spending your money, ask for a wasted-spend review; that's
`adspirer-optimize`.
